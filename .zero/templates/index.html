Terms of Service

1. Self-custody. Zero is a self-custodial wallet. Your recovery phrase and password never leave your device. We cannot recover them.

2. No financial advice. Zero does not provide investment, legal, or tax advice.

3. Irreversible transactions. Blockchain transactions cannot be undone. Verify addresses carefully.

4. No warranty. The software is provided "as is" under the MIT license. Use at your own risk.

Privacy Policy

Zero-knowledge by design. Your recovery phrase, private keys, and password are generated and used entirely in your browser. They are never transmitted to any server.

Local storage only. Your encrypted wallet is stored in this browser's IndexedDB. It is protected by your password (Argon2id + AES-256-GCM).

Network requests. The wallet talks only to its own server, which forwards balance, price, history, and signed-transaction requests to public Solana RPC nodes and CoinGecko. No keys are sent.

No tracking. We use no cookies, analytics, or trackers.
