fingerprintGenerative Fingerprint — identity art

Type any name and a tiny generator paints its unique artwork: the name is hashed into a random-looking (but perfectly repeatable) seed that drives thousands of brush strokes. The same name always produces the exact same picture on every device — nothing is stored, nothing is sent.
