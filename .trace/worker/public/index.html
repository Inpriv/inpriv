help What am I looking at?
IP check — shows the public address and network every site sees. If you are on a VPN, this should be the VPN's address, tagged as VPN/datacenter.
DNS leak — your browser resolves domains somewhere. If that resolver egresses outside your VPN/proxy network (different ISP or country), sites can infer who resolves for you: a DNS leak.
WebRTC leak — browsers build direct peer connections (calls, file sharing) and may list your real interface IPs in the process. If a residential IP appears here while you use a VPN, your origin is leaking.
Privacy note — probes go directly from your browser to public APIs (ipwho.is, ipapi.co, Cloudflare/Google DNS). Nothing is logged or stored by this page.
Fixes — enable your VPN's DNS/WebRTC protection, use uBlock Origin's "Prevent WebRTC from leaking local IP" or browser flags, and re-test.
