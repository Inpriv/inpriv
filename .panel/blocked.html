Region Blocked

Due to the European Union's Chat Control 2.0 regulation — which mandates mass surveillance of private communications — Inpriv cannot provide services to visitors from the European Union. This regulation fundamentally violates the right to privacy and anonymous communication.

Inpriv's Stance
Your privacy matters. Inpriv does not comply with mass surveillance mandates. We believe in end-to-end encryption, anonymous access, and zero data collection. Until EU regulations respect these fundamental rights, we are unable to serve users from the European region.
