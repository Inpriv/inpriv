Privacy Policy

What Inpriv stores, what it never stores, how long it keeps things, and who else handles your data.

Effective 17 October 2026

The short version

  • Mail between Inpriv users is end-to-end encrypted in your browser. We can't read it.
  • Mail from or to other providers (Gmail, Outlook…) passes through our mail relay in readable form; incoming mail is then stored encrypted to your key.
  • Subject lines, sender and recipient addresses and times are not end-to-end encrypted. We need them to deliver and list your mail.
  • Temp inboxes are not encrypted and are deleted about 24 hours after creation.
  • We keep an access log (IP address, country, network, browser, time, what happened) for 90 days, to stop abuse and answer valid legal requests.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no data sales. The code is open source, so you can check all of this.

1. Who we are

"Inpriv", "we" and "us" mean the operator of the Inpriv services at inpriv.xyz, mail.inpriv.xyz, temp.inpriv.xyz and id.inpriv.xyz. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Questions and requests: privacy@inpriv.xyz.

2. What we never have

  • The content of mail between Inpriv users. It's encrypted in the sender's browser with the recipient's public key (RSA-OAEP 2048 + AES-256-GCM) before it reaches us.
  • The content of stored mail from outside senders after it's been received. It's encrypted to your public key on arrival, and only your browser can open it.
  • Your password. We store only a salted hash (PBKDF2-SHA-256, 300,000 iterations).
  • Your mailbox private key or the key your password unlocks it with. Both exist only in your browser.
  • Anything you type into a page and don't submit, and any analytics, advertising or tracking data. We don't run analytics or ads.

Because we can't read this content, we also can't recover it. If you forget your password, mail encrypted to your old key can't be opened again.

Mail to and from other providers

  • Incoming: mail from Gmail, Outlook and others arrives over normal email, unencrypted, through our mail provider Resend. Our server encrypts it to your public key before storing it. While it's received, it's readable to Resend and to our server.
  • Outgoing: mail you send to other providers is handed to Resend as normal email. It's readable to Resend, to the recipient's provider, and to our server while it's sent. An encrypted copy is kept in your Sent box.

5. Inpriv Temp

  • The inbox: its random address, a hash of the access token your browser holds, and created and expiry times.
  • Messages: sender, recipient, subject, text and HTML body, attachments (up to 512 KB each, 2 MB per message), size and time. These are stored unencrypted, so don't use Temp for anything sensitive.
  • Deletion: everything is deleted when the inbox expires, 24 hours after it's created. Cleanup runs every hour. Messages are never kept longer than 7 days in any case. You can also delete an inbox yourself at any time.
  • Logging: creating an inbox is written to the access log (section 6).

6. Access log

To fight abuse and to answer valid legal requests, we keep an access log. Each entry has:

  • the time, the service, and what happened, for example "Mail sign-in" or "message sent";
  • the account (username or Temp address);
  • for sent mail, the recipient address;
  • the full IP address, the country and network (ASN) derived from it, and the browser's user-agent string.

It's written when you create, sign in to or change your Inpriv ID (failed sign-ins included), sign in to Mail, create a mailbox, send a message, or create a Temp inbox. Entries are deleted after 90 days, including after you delete your account. The access log never contains message content, passwords or keys.

7. On your device

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The sites store only what they need to work:

  • id.inpriv.xyz: a sign-in cookie (HttpOnly, Secure). Other Inpriv sites ask id.inpriv.xyz whether you're signed in, for Quick Sign-In.
  • Mail: your session token. With "Skip master password" on, also a device key and a random device id. Plus your theme and whether to show the "Mailbox unlocked" message.
  • Temp: the token for your current inbox.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.

8. Mail you send to us

Messages to our team addresses (support@, abuse@, dmca@, legal@, privacy@, security@, admin@, hello@) are stored as received, so we can answer them, and kept until we delete them. They aren't end-to-end encrypted.

11. When we share data

Only:

  • with the providers in section 9, to run the service;
  • when the law requires it, in answer to valid legal process. See the Law Enforcement Guidelines for what can and can't be provided. We notify affected users unless the law forbids it or it would put someone in danger.
  • to protect someone's life or physical safety in an emergency;
  • to investigate and stop abuse of Inpriv, as described in the Acceptable Use Policy.

12. Legal bases

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • contract: your account, mailbox, messages, sessions and settings, which we need to provide the service you signed up for;
  • legitimate interests: the access log, security events and rate limits, to keep the service secure and free of abuse. We limit them to what's needed and delete them after 90 days.
  • legal obligation: responding to valid legal requests and keeping data a preservation request covers;
  • consent: the optional recovery email, which you can remove at any time.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct it;
  • delete it (you can delete your account yourself in your account settings on id.inpriv.xyz);
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • hand it to you in a portable format.

Write to privacy@inpriv.xyz from your @inpriv.xyz address, or say how we can verify you. We answer within one month.

We can't hand over mail content we can't read, but you can open and copy it yourself while you're signed in. You also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

14. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, 2FA secrets are encrypted at rest, and session tokens are stored as hashes.
  • Mail between Inpriv users is end-to-end encrypted.
  • The admin panel needs a separate authenticator code, and every lookup of user records in it is logged.

If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires. Found a vulnerability? See SECURITY.md.

15. Children

Inpriv isn't for children under 16. We don't knowingly hold data about them. If you think a child has an account, write to privacy@inpriv.xyz and we'll delete it.

16. Changes

We'll update this page when what we store changes, and change the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also give notice on the site before they take effect. The full history is public in the source repository.
