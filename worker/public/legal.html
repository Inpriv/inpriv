Inpriv in one paragraph

Inpriv is a small, open-source email service on inpriv.xyz, run on Cloudflare. Inpriv Mail encrypts mail between Inpriv users in the sender's browser, so the server can't read it. Inpriv Temp gives you a throwaway address for 24 hours; it isn't end-to-end encrypted. Inpriv ID is the one account behind Mail. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers. To handle abuse and valid legal requests, Inpriv keeps a limited access log (IP address, time, browser) for 90 days. The Privacy Policy lists every piece of data that's kept.
