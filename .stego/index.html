edit_note

1. Write & lock

Pick a PNG, type your secret and an optional password. The message is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM.

hide_image

2. Embed

The ciphertext is spread across the least-significant bits of the pixels. The result looks identical — and metadata is stripped.

visibility

3. Share & reveal

Send the PNG as a file. Anyone with Stego — and the password, if you set one — can reveal the message.

Part of Inpriv — zero-knowledge utilities by Inpriv Labs. Share images as files — compressing or re-encoding (JPEG, screenshots, messengers) destroys the hidden data.
