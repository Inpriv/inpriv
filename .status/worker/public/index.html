How we check

  • Every 5 minutes, day and night. Cloudflare's network opens each site's health page, and for Mail, Temp and ID also reads from its database.
  • Down means no healthy answer within 8 seconds, twice in a row. Slow means the answer took longer than 1.5 seconds.
  • Maintenance is when we paused a site on purpose. It doesn't count against uptime. Checks are kept for 90 days.
  • Nothing about you. This page loads nothing from other sites, and we keep no record of who looks at it.
